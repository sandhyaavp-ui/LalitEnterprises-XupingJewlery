Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Lalit Enterprises ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our enquiry form, appointment request form, video call booking, or chatbot, we may collect: your name, phone number, email address (where provided), location, and any message or notes you submit. We also use Google Analytics, which collects anonymised usage data such as pages visited and general location, to help us understand how our website is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and process wholesale orders
  • Schedule and conduct video call consultations or appointments
  • Contact you via phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding your enquiry or order
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may process data on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics
  • Cloudinary — hosting for product images
  • WhatsApp — when you choose to contact us or receive communication via WhatsApp

5. Data Storage and Security

Information you submit is stored securely in our database, hosted on Railway's infrastructure. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by us. To exercise these rights, contact us at 098410 66880 or via our Contact page.

7. Cookies

This website uses cookies via Google Analytics to understand website usage. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at 098410 66880, or via the enquiry form on our Contact page, or by mail at 39/2 EK, Agraharam St, Edapalaiyam, Park Town, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600003.
