Our Story

Two decades supplying India's jewellery resellers.

Lalit Enterprises has operated as an authorised Xuping jewellery agent out of Chennai since 2002, supplying wholesale everyday-wear imitation jewellery to resellers, boutique owners and retailers across India.

We built this business on a simple idea: resellers do better with a supplier who shows up consistently — same quality, same fair pricing, same stock reliability, order after order — rather than one who treats every sale as a one-off.

That's why we offer things larger, faceless suppliers don't: 1-on-1 video call consultations to see the actual pieces before you buy, direct WhatsApp access to a real person, and dispatch from Chennai within 24–48 hours of confirmation.
